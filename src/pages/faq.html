Orders

How do I place an order?

Add what you love to your cart, press Checkout, and fill in your details. You do not need an account.

Can I change my order after I place it?

Write to us as soon as you can and we will try to help before it ships. Once it is printed we cannot change it.

What can I pay with?

Checkout shows the payment choices that are available, like major credit and debit cards. Payments go through a secure payment company, and we never see your full card number. [Confirm after the payment account is set up.]

Shipping & returns

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–3 business days and arrive about 3–5 days after that. [Confirm with the printer.]

Where do you ship?

The United States for now, with more places coming later.

What if my package is lost?

Tell us and we will look into it and send a new one if we need to.

How do I return something?

Tell us within 30 days of when it arrives. See the Shipping & Returns page for the steps.

Products

What are the stickers made of?

Sturdy, waterproof vinyl that is great for water bottles and laptops. [Example answer: confirm once we pick a printer.]

What sizes do the shirts come in?

See the Size Guide for measurements. If you are between sizes, pick the bigger one for a comfy fit.

How do I wash my shirt or sweatshirt?

Turn it inside out, wash it cold and tumble dry low to keep the print looking cute. [Confirm with the printer’s care instructions.]

About CuteMerch

Who makes CuteMerch?

CuteMerch is a small, family-run shop built around one artist’s hand-drawn characters.

Are the drawings really hand drawn?

Yes! Every character is drawn and colored by hand, then scanned so we can put it on our products.

Can I suggest a new character?

We love ideas, but we are still building a safe way to share them. For now, please do not send personal details or photos of children.
