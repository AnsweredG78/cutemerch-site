About CuteMerch

Who makes CuteMerch?

CuteMerch is a small, family-run shop built around one artist’s hand-drawn characters.

Are the drawings really hand drawn?

Yes! Every character is drawn and colored by hand, then scanned so we can put it on our products.

Can I suggest a new character?

We love ideas, but we are still building a safe way to share them. For now, please do not send personal details or photos of children.
