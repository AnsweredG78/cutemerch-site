Returns

If something is wrong with your order, let us know within 30 days of when it arrives.

  1. Write to us. Use the Contact page and include your order number.
  2. Tell us what happened. Send a quick note, and a photo if something arrived damaged.
  3. We fix it. We will send a new one or give your money back.

Quick answers

Can I change my order after I place it?

Write to us as soon as you can and we will try to help before it ships.

What if my package is lost?

Tell us and we will look into it and send a new one if we need to.

Can I return a sticker I already stuck on something?

If it is not what you wanted, write to us and we will talk about it. We want you to be happy!
