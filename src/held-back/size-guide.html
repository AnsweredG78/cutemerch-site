How to measure

  1. Chest. Lay a shirt you already love flat. Measure straight across, just under the armpits.
  2. Length. Measure from the top of the shoulder, next to the collar, down to the bottom hem.
  3. Compare. Find the size in the tables below that is closest to your numbers.
